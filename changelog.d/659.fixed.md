The `--notify <mode>` and `--open-links <bool>` space forms are now parsed as flags on `run` instead of being
 forwarded to the agent: previously the documented form silently did the opposite (the notify watcher stayed
 active) and the bare word (`off`, `false`) leaked into the agent's arguments. Both the space and `=` forms work,
 and every pflag boolean spelling of `--open-links` (`0`/`1`/`t`/`f`, case variants) is recognised.
