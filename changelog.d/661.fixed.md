A live `--memory` decrease the runtime does not apply is now reported as not applied, instead of being reported as
 applied while the guest keeps the larger memory. The resize wait is direction-relative: a raise polls until the
 live limit reaches at least the requested value (a larger limit another client holds still satisfies it), a decrease
 is reported not applied from the first read, and a metrics read error is surfaced as an unconfirmed read rather than
 as not applied.
