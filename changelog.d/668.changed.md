Internal: the recommended CLI tools in the managed base image are updated. Added:
`lsof`, `strace`, `gnupg`, `openssl`, and Python's `venv`/`pip` (so `python3 -m venv` works). Dropped:
`net-tools` (use `ip`/`ss`) and `recode` (use `iconv`). Replaced: `uuid` by `uuid-runtime` (which provides
`uuidgen`) and `bsdmainutils` by `bsdextrautils`. `fuse-overlayfs` now relies on apt to pull its `libfuse3-4`
dependency instead of listing it.
