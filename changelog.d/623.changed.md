`make check` and `make verify` are faster: their independent targets now run as parallel jobs by
  default (`JOBS=<n>` overrides the count), and `ci/test-check-deadcode.sh` builds the deadcode
  checker once and shares it through a new `DEADCODE_CHECKER` seam instead of rebuilding it for every
  fixture case (the `deadcode-test` step alone drops from ~12s to ~3s).
