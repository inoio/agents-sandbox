Learn mode: the after-run names the hosts whose TLS handshake through the learn proxy failed (a pinned client or one
missing the sandbox CA trust) — charged to the tunnel under the connection target, so a failure a no-SNI client may
have caused is never blamed on a named host sharing its address — and, when the proxy logged problems, its log under
the state directory's `learn/proxy-logs/`, which holds no header or query values and expires after 30 days (#815).
