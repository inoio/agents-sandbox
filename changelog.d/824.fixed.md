Learn mode reports failed TLS handshakes without counting an all-failed tunnel as verified service use. The proxy uses
goproxy's session ID to attribute a failure to its tunnel; missing or reused IDs stay unattributed rather than blaming
a host that shares an IP. The egress proposal keeps the host as a conservative fallback because pinning and missing CA
trust are indistinguishable to the proxy. Proxy logs use bounded, value-free warning summaries in unique files under
`learn/proxy-logs/`; they report truncation and write failures and expire after 30 days (#815).
