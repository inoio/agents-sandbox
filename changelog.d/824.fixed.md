Learn mode: the after-run names the host a failed TLS handshake through the learn proxy can be attributed to (a pinned
client or one missing the sandbox CA trust) — charged to the tunnel under the connection target, so a failure a no-SNI
client may have caused is never blamed on a named host sharing its address, and a failure is left unattributed when
several entries share the target — and, when the proxy logged problems, its log under
the state directory's `learn/proxy-logs/`, which holds no header or query values and expires after 30 days (#815).
