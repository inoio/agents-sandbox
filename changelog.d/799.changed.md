Made the hermetic envelope's strict (`--netns`) tier green: the uid/permission fixtures that assumed an
unprivileged uid now induce their read, write, and removal failures through package seams instead of
`chmod`, so they hold under the tier's uid 0. This covers the six fixtures the inventory recorded
(`configmerge` mirror read, `runtimemigration` volume listing, the three `vm` state-write paths) and the
`pruning`/`state` sweep fixtures written since: `state.removeSweepEntry` and the `pruning` sweep seams
join the existing `state.OverrideStateWriteForTest` hook. The envelope also pins `GOTOOLCHAIN=local`, so a
`toolchain` directive cannot trigger a download inside the network namespace. Developer-only change (#799).
