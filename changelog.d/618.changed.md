`gopls`/`goimports`/`ast-grep` for navigation and refactoring, and puts `~/go/bin` on `PATH`; the

`scripts/upstream-drift.sh` and `scripts/rebase-status.sh` (each with a skill), report how far

requires a fragment's slug to be the PR number.
