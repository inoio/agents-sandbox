The `pi` drop-in copy no longer copies credentials into the VM. It now copies only the credential-free files in
`~/.pi/agent` (settings, keybindings, instruction files, system prompts, and the `extensions/`, `skills/`, `prompts/`,
and `themes/` trees); `auth.json`, `models.json`, `mcp.json`, `mcp-auth.json`, and sessions stay on the host. If you
relied on the copied login, log in inside the VM or move the key to `env.secret.yaml`. Copies made by earlier
versions stay in an existing home volume until you delete them (`rm ~/.pi/agent/auth.json ~/.pi/agent/models.json`
inside the VM) or remove the volume (#750).
